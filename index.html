Format
How dating works: The galaxy’s calendar pivots on the Battle of Yavin — the destruction of the first Death Star in A New Hope (0). Everything earlier counts down in BBY; everything later counts up in ABY. Placement follows in-universe events, so a 2021 novel can sit centuries before a 1999 film.
